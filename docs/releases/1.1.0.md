# 1.1.0 review calibration

Status: **candidate 2026-10-14**. Instruction-only changes to the three review
tiers plus one new reference file. No state format change (`format_version: 3`
and the five-field finding grammar are unchanged), no new runtime dependency, and
the lint, hooks, kickoff and resume are unchanged.

## Why

Ask a frontier model to find bugs and it finds some; ask again after fixing them
and it finds more. Many are real, many are unreachable edge cases, pre-existing
issues or taste. The itworks user cannot tell which, so every overblown finding
costs them an accept-or-fix decision they are not equipped to make, and enough
of them teach them to ignore the tool. The research behind this release, with
sources, is in `docs/research/2026-10-14-llm-review-calibration.md`; the short
version is that Anthropic's own review tooling and the practitioner write-ups
agree on the same handful of rules, and itworks stated almost none of them.

## Behavior changes

- **One rule source.** `references/review-calibration.md` holds the calibration
  rules; the three tiers, the audit method and the five lens rubrics point at it.
- **The mode is stated.** Checkpoint and closeout review for precision: a runbook
  step that finds nothing records "clean" with its command and output. Audit lane
  reviewers review for recall and never self-censor; their verifiers review for
  precision and never speculate.
- **Zero is a valid answer.** An empty report with a full coverage list is a
  complete report at every tier and profile. Nobody is asked for more findings
  after an empty result; there is no floor.
- **Every finding names its trigger and who can reach it.** A suspicion with no
  trigger is dropped at dedup; a finding with no "reachable by" is not written.
- **The do-not-flag list** (outside scope, caught by typecheck or lint or the lens
  greps, settled, intentional, taste, no rubric rung) is checked before anything
  is emitted.
- **Reachability decides disposition.** An impossible trigger, with the guard
  quoted, is a refutation. An unrealistic actor is ADVISORY at most. Rare but
  realistic keeps its rung. The money, time and permission exception is unchanged.
- **PLAUSIBLE.** A verifier can now answer PLAUSIBLE: mechanism traced with exact
  values, trigger realistic but not producible here. It is recorded as an open
  finding whose evidence to close is the exact probe, capped at IMPORTANT unless
  the exception applies, and it does not keep the audit INCOMPLETE. Before this
  release such items were "unresolved" and could freeze `last_audit` indefinitely.
- **Dedup and tally.** Suspicions are folded by line and mechanism before
  verification; the audit scope line records raised, deduplicated, confirmed,
  plausible, partial, refuted and unresolved counts.
- **Evidence hygiene.** A cited file, line, function or commit that does not exist
  refutes the item; a reviewer that does it twice in a lane is re-run fresh.
- **Re-reviews are gap sweeps.** An audit of an unchanged sha runs the gap-sweep
  brief: previous findings first, only what is new, nothing refuted or accepted
  re-raised, empty when nothing is new.
- **What the user hears.** CRITICAL and IMPORTANT in full, ADVISORY as a count,
  every line still in REVIEWS.md.
- **Recurrences become rules.** A finding type seen at three checkpoints of one
  project gets a one-line proposal for a deterministic check in its place.

## Upgrading

Nothing to migrate. Existing REVIEWS.md files are read as before; new audit
sections may carry `plausible, not reproduced here:` descriptions and a tally in
the scope line, both inside the existing grammar.
